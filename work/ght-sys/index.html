GHT-SYS

A company that builds and operates information technology and systems.

Visual identity and campaign for an IT company · 2023

Within a few years the company became involved internationally across most areas of IT development. The identity was made to support that growth.

An anthracite base carries two strong complementary colours, and the typeface is tuned to programming and printed circuit boards. The system runs from business cards through work attire to the campaign posters.
